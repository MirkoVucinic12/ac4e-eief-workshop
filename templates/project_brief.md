# Project brief template

> **Not on the workshop path.** Kept for longer projects after the workshop. For the workshop, use [`agent_task_contract.md`](agent_task_contract.md) (task card) and [`issue_card.md`](issue_card.md).

Copy this into `docs/project_brief.md` or adapt the existing file.

1. Research question
2. Contribution (empirical, teaching, or methods; state which)
3. Data or model inputs (source, unit of observation, and whether public, restricted, or confidential)
4. Method (estimand in words; weights, standard errors, and clustering if any)
5. What you will finish in this workshop
6. Acceptance criteria (commands to run and what the researcher checks)

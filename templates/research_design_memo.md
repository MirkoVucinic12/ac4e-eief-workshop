# Research design memo template

> **Not on the workshop path.** Kept for longer projects after the workshop. For the workshop, use [`agent_task_contract.md`](agent_task_contract.md) (task card) and [`issue_card.md`](issue_card.md).

1. Research question
2. Literature (verified sources only; attach the citation ledger)
3. Hypotheses or theoretical predictions
4. Data or model inputs (source, unit of observation, and sample restrictions; do not modify raw files)
5. Empirical specification or model solution method
6. Identification, assumptions, or equilibrium conditions (written and signed off by the researcher)
7. Planned tables, figures, and code-to-output map
8. Limitations
